# Nobody logo exploration prompts

These are the exact prompt components used with the built-in image generation tool. Each request concatenates Common, a newline, and one candidate paragraph. The designs are exploratory; no final identity has been approved.

## Common

```text
Use case: logo-brand.
Create a polished exploratory logo presentation for "Nobody", a quietly resident macOS/Windows developer utility for translating human language while preserving code, and transferring text/files across nearby computers. Brand attitude: every person's ideas have value regardless of their language or status; modest presence, confident individuality, respectful and useful. Audience: independent developers. This is one candidate concept, not a final approved identity.
Design a precise flat graphic logo with a memorable silhouette, broad solid strokes and clear negative space, capable of working as a 20px monochrome menu-bar symbol as well as a desktop icon. Restrained deep forest green #244A3B, warm ivory #F6F4ED and charcoal. No gradients, 3D, shadows, glows, texture, flags, globes, circuit nodes, AI sparkles, robots, translation A/文 symbols or stock brand clipart. Original design, do not imitate any existing company logo.
Square high-resolution composition: very generous warm ivory space; one oversized dark primary symbol centered in the upper half; a carefully spaced, humanist sans-serif wordmark "Nobody" centered below. Bottom strip contains three small demonstrations of the SAME exact mark: black-on-white, ivory-on-forest square app icon, and a tiny monochrome mark. Do not add long descriptions or extra slogans. Precise optical balance and clear readable typography. Use only the word "Nobody" plus the small candidate label specified below.
```

## A / EQUAL

```text
Candidate A / EQUAL. Invent a strong, compact uppercase N monogram built from TWO equally weighted softened vertical forms and one generous diagonal negative-space passage between them. The forms meet on equal terms; neither one dominates. The silhouette must still read immediately as N, not a chain link, infinity sign, lightning bolt or crypto token. Slightly softened external corners with sharper internal cuts give it a quiet but distinctive typographic personality. Main silhouette nearly square. Small label at top left exactly "A / EQUAL".
```

## B / HUMAN

```text
Candidate B / HUMAN. Invent a warm, anonymous abstract human emblem using one solid circular head and ONE unusually shaped broad, open arch for the shoulders/body. The shoulder shape subtly suggests a lowercase n, with a deliberate open negative-space entrance on one side. Give it an unmistakable individual silhouette rather than a generic user-account bust. No eyes, face, hair, hat, hood, gender, mask or cultural identifiers. Equal dignity and self-possession, not secrecy or surveillance. Keep the mark geometrically spare, almost like a little signature someone could remember and draw. Main silhouette slightly taller than wide. Small label at top left exactly "B / HUMAN".
```

## C / VOICES

```text
Candidate C / VOICES. Invent an abstract emblem made from TWO mirrored, equally sized solid quotation-like forms facing toward each other, each a rounded block with one crisp inward cut. Their shared negative space becomes an open, gently stepped passage from left to right: different voices, one continuous thought. A balanced, compact horizontal silhouette with strong inner space; distinct from a letter N and distinct from a person. Do not draw literal chat bubbles, a link icon, infinity sign or arrows. Conceptual editorial design, reductive yet inviting. Small label at top left exactly "C / VOICES".
```
